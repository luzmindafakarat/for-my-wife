For My Beautiful Baby

hi baby, i just want to take this opportunity to thank you for being such a strong and brave woman
i want you to know that i am always proud of you, for making it this far. i know its not that easy
but look at you, you made it here. watching you grow through challenges, push forward with passion,
and still carry your beautiful smile it’s something that inspires me every single day you’ve faced so much,
yet you keep going, and it makes my heart overflow with admiration for your strength and resilience.
you never give up, even when you’re tired, and that determination of yours makes me love you
even more each time. no matter what happens, you always find a way to rise again, and that’s a rare,
powerful thing to witness. i hope you realize how incredible you are.
